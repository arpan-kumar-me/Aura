# AURA — Your Health, Connected

> **Your health is a story, not a snapshot.**

AURA is a longitudinal health intelligence platform designed to help people connect the dots across medical records, laboratory results, symptoms, and health events over time.

Instead of viewing every medical report in isolation, AURA aims to build a connected health story that makes historical changes easier to understand and helps patients prepare for more informed conversations with their clinicians.

## The Problem

Health information is often scattered across laboratory reports, prescriptions, hospital visits, and different healthcare providers.

This fragmentation can make it difficult to:

- Understand how health measurements change over time.
- Recall important details from previous consultations.
- Present a coherent medical history to a new clinician.
- Distinguish meaningful changes from isolated results.

Most systems focus on individual encounters or records. AURA explores how these records can be understood together across time.

## Our Solution

AURA transforms disconnected health information into a structured, chronological health story.

### Key Features

- **Health Story:** Explore medical events and records on a unified timeline.
- **Biomarker Trends:** Visualise changes in laboratory measurements over time.
- **Connected Records:** Bring different types of health information into one interface.
- **Longitudinal Insights:** Explore potential temporal patterns with a focus on traceability and context.
- **Connected Sources:** View the sources associated with a patient's health information.
- **Clinician-Ready Summaries:** Work towards making complex histories easier to review and discuss during consultations.

## How It Works

1. **Collect** — Bring together relevant medical records and health information.
2. **Structure** — Organise events, dates, measurements, and extracted information.
3. **Connect** — Examine changes and potential temporal relationships across records.
4. **Review** — Present findings with supporting evidence so patients and clinicians can evaluate them.

AURA is designed to distinguish temporal associations from causal claims. Any potential finding should be reviewed in context and should not be treated as a diagnosis.

## Technology

AURA's prototype explores a modern web-based architecture for presenting and analysing longitudinal health information.

The development stack includes React, TypeScript, and Vite. AI-assisted extraction and interpretation, backend services, persistent storage, and healthcare integrations depend on the current implementation and configuration.
